Privacy Policy

Application: Mobile Repair Shop (Android App com.aistudio.mobilerepair.kzptqr & Web POS)
Effective Date: November 22, 2026 · Contact: parasarasadam@gmail.com

Mobile Repair Shop is a repair-counter management tool built for mobile repair technicians and shop owners. We designed the app with a strict user-owned, zero-external-server architecture: your business records belong solely to you.

1. Data Stored Locally on Your Device

Repair job cards, customer names and phone numbers, device condition checklists, Khata credit/payment ledgers, and shop profile settings are stored locally on your Android device (Room database) or in your web browser's local storage so the app works offline.

2. Google Account & Google API Data Usage

Connecting your Google Account is completely optional and used strictly to synchronize and back up your repair shop records directly to your own Google Drive. When you choose to connect, the app requests only these two OAuth scopes:

  • https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive.file — Used to create and manage the app's dedicated Mobile Repair Shop folder, spreadsheet (MobileRepairShop_DB), and scheduled backup snapshots in your own Google Drive.
  • https://www.googleapis.com/auth/spreadsheets — Used to read and write your job cards, shop profile, and audit logs to your linked MobileRepairShop_DB Google Sheet for 2-way sync between your Android phone and Desktop Web POS.

3. Google API Services User Data Policy (Limited Use Disclosure)

Mobile Repair Shop's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy , including the Limited Use requirements.

  • We do not run any central backend server that collects, stores, or views your Google Drive or Google Sheets data.
  • We do not sell, rent, share, or transfer your Google user data to third parties or advertisers.
  • We do not use Google user data for advertising, profiling, or training AI/ML models.
  • OAuth access tokens in the Web POS are kept strictly in memory and cleared when you disconnect or close the session.

4. Third-Party Services & Permissions

  • WhatsApp Sharing: When you tap Send on WhatsApp, the app opens WhatsApp on your device with your pre-filled repair status message or generated PDF receipt. Nothing is sent automatically without your action.
  • Notifications (Android): Used locally on your device to alert you about new job cards, stage updates, nightly Google Drive backup results, and available app updates.
  • GitHub Update Check: The Android app may fetch public release metadata from GitHub to check if a newer app version is available. No personal shop or customer data is transmitted.

5. Data Retention, Revocation & Deletion

Because all data lives either locally on your device or inside your personal Google Drive, you remain in complete control at all times:

  • Disconnect in-app: Open Settings → Sync Manager → Disconnect to stop syncing immediately.
  • Revoke Google access: You can revoke the app's permissions anytime at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
  • Delete data: Delete your local records or uninstall the app, and delete the Mobile Repair Shop folder / MobileRepairShop_DB spreadsheet directly in your Google Drive.

6. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy or how Mobile Repair Shop handles data, contact us at: parasarasadam@gmail.com.
